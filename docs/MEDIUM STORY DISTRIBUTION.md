Short answer first, then the reasoning:

Best time to publish on Medium for maximum reach:
Tuesday or Wednesday, between 8:00–10:00 AM Eastern Time

Now let me explain why that window matters and how to squeeze the most out of it.

The Medium Daily Digest Reality (How Articles Get Picked)

Medium Daily Digest is not “viral in 2 hours” content.
It’s curated + momentum-based.

What Medium’s system looks for in the first 24–48 hours:

Early read-through (people finish the article)

Highlights (readers selecting text)

Claps (especially from Medium members)

Saves / follows

Low bounce rate

The goal of timing is to maximize quality engagement early, not raw clicks.

Why Tuesday–Wednesday Works Best
🚫 Monday

People are catching up

Lower completion rates

Skimming behavior

🚫 Friday

Attention drops fast

Weekend reading patterns are inconsistent

✅ Tuesday & Wednesday

Readers are settled into the week

More long-form reading

Higher completion and highlight rates

This is especially true for engineering / dev audience pieces.

Why Morning Matters (But Not Too Early)
Ideal window:

8:00–10:00 AM ET
(5:00–7:00 AM PT)

Why:

East Coast professionals read before work

West Coast catches it early morning

Europe sees it mid-day

Publishing earlier than 7 AM ET risks:

Low initial engagement

Algorithm misclassification

Later than 11 AM ET:

Misses prime attention window

The “Slow Burn” Advantage of Your Article

This piece is not clickbait, which is a good thing.

It’s:

Thoughtful

Experience-based

High-signal

Those articles tend to:

Start slow

Build over 24–72 hours

Get picked up in Daily Digest 2–5 days later

Your goal is to give Medium confidence, not a spike.

What to Do Right After You Publish (Critical)
Within the First 2 Hours

Do not blast it everywhere.

Instead:

Share it with 3–5 thoughtful readers you trust

Ask them to:

Read fully

Highlight at least one sentence

Leave a short response if they genuinely feel it

This trains Medium’s system to see it as “high-quality reading”.

Where to Share (Selective, Not Loud)

After ~12–24 hours:

Expo / React Native Discord (in a discussion channel)

A thoughtful post on X or LinkedIn:

“I wasn’t trying to publish my app — I was just trying to test it. This experience changed how I think about developer tooling.”

No hashtags. No pitch.
